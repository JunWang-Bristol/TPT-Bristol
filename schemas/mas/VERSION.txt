NOTE: MAS $refs a sibling spec, PEAS, which is NOT part of the MAS repo.
The MAS project's own scripts/validate-samples.py builds its registry from
("schemas", "../PEAS/schemas"), i.e. a PEAS checkout beside MAS.  Without it,
four URI prefixes are unresolvable:
    https://psma.com/peas/utils.json
    https://psma.com/peas/inputs/operatingPointExcitation.json
    https://psma.com/peas/outputs/insulationCoordination.json
    https://psma.com/peas/outputs/lossElementPerHarmonic.json
opentpt.mas_schema stubs those and reports which subtrees went unchecked.
Pass --peas <dir> to validate completely.
